The new `@fluixi/di` package provides typed tokens with factory and scope, useValue/useClass/useFactory/useExisting and multi providers, optional/self/skipSelf/host injection options, constructor injection from the holding injector, detailed `InjectionError` with resolution path, `DestroyRef` and `INJECTOR`, `providePlatform`, request-root disposal, `provideAppInitializer` and `runAppInitializers`, `isInInjectionContext` and `assertInInjectionContext`, and the testing utilities `createTestInjector` and `resetInjection` in `@fluixi/di/testing`.
