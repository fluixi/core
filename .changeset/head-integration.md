Wire @fluixi/head into the framework (zero-config SEO). renderRequestAsync collects seo()/useHead metadata during SSR and carries it back; @fluixi/start lifts it into <head>/<html> for SSR + prerender. startClient mounts the head registry so it hydrates (server tags reused by key) and updates live, including on route transitions (seo() auto-disposes on owner unmount). Apps just call seo({...}) — no setup.
