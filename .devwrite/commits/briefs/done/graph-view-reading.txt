  1. a store folds into one card with a count of what is inside, and opens a level at a time:
     the store, then the objects in it, then their keys. Edges into a folded node are drawn
     to the card, so a memo reading cart.total still visibly reads something.
  2. hovering a node dims what it cannot reach and lights what it can, following where values
     travel. Ownership is one hop only — a component owns most of a page, and following it
     lights everything, which is the same as lighting nothing.
  3. wheel zooms about the cursor, drag pans, double-click resets. The pointer is captured
     only once a drag passes the slop threshold: capturing on pointerdown retargets the click
     that follows, and nothing could be selected.
  4. a propagation is staged rather than flashed. The nodes that changed together are ordered
     along the edges between them, a step apart, and each edge lights while the value is
     crossing it. Flashing them together says these changed; a step apart says which one
     caused which.
