        inspector, and draws a component node as a component. The inspector redraws only
        when what it shows has changed.

        it, and shows nothing when a node has none. Where the metadata comes from is
        devtools' business.
        the inspector rebuilt its markup on every frame, sixty times a second, to redraw
        what was usually the same thing.

        location shows no source line at all
