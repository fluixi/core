files:  playground/src/run.ts, src/preview/bootstrap.ts, src/link.ts, src/Playground.tsx,
        src/styles.css
what:   the preview is an iframe holding the project's own document, and the framework loads
        inside it.
why:    the preview was the body of index.html injected into a div, so the head, any style
        element and everything on the body tag were dropped — style="background: red" there
        did nothing at all. An iframe is the honest shape: the project's html is the document
        and its script is the entry, the way vite would serve it.
        the framework has to load in that realm rather than be handed in. Event delegation
        attaches to document, so a runtime taken from the outer page would bind its listeners
        there and the preview would render and then ignore every click. The reactor lives
        there too, and the page reads the graph back out.
verify: a body background, a head stylesheet and a click all take effect
