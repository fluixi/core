files:  packages/compiler/src/transform/source-locations.ts (new), transform/templates.ts,
        transform/source-locations.spec.ts (new)
what:   a `sourceLocations` option records where each reactive primitive and each component
        was written, as a statement in front of the one that creates the node. Off by
        default, so a production build carries none of it.
why:    the devtools graph could say what a node is and what it depends on, but not where it
        came from — the one question you actually have when a node misbehaves.
        the mark goes in front of the statement rather than round the call, because the call
        may already be being rewritten and two passes editing one span is a splice over a
        splice. It calls a global rather than importing devtools, so the author's modules do
        not each take a dependency to carry a debug string.
        positions come from the original source, before any pass moves anything, so they
        point at what was written rather than at generated output.
        `outermost` now keeps a zero-width insertion sitting on an edit's boundary: an
        insertion there is beside the edit, not folded into it, and dropping it lost the
        mark for `$effect(…)` and for a component whose arrow body ends where its jsx does.
verify: six new specs; the compiler's own suite still passes
